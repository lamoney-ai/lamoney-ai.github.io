About Us

Strategy and technology for serious market participants

La-Money Financial Services Pvt Ltd is a Mumbai-based consultancy focused on algorithmic trading and options strategies across the Indian derivatives and commodity markets.

We provide technology support to investors, clients, traders and jobbers. We help them design their trading strategies and build the execution algorithms that run them, with the aim of improving consistency and precision in execution.

The company is led by Khushboo Purohit and Girish Purohit, who together bring decades of hands-on experience in capital markets.
